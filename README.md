Here in you can drive in any location of the world and see the local time and weather and listen local radio in real time.

1. Extract files.
2. Install Node.js here https://nodejs.org/
3. Make an account in Cesium and get a token here https://ion.cesium.com/tokens
4. Start the game clicking on Iniciar World Auto.bat file.
5. Wait some minutes until a browser window is open and you will see the game in http://localhost:8000/World%20Auto.html
6. Enter the Cesium token (this is just once the first time you run the script).
7. Press W to move forward if the loading of 3d buildings gets too slow or move the mouse up and down (it's something I have to fix yet).
8. You drive the car with W-S-D-A keys.
9. You can get out and in from your car with F.
10. Press R to listen local radio and N to change the station.
11. You cand turn on and off the car lights with B.
13. Press P to open the Autopilot (you can just add the destination and the car will start from your current location).

This is just a software to test.
